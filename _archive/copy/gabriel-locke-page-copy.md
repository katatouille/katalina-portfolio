## PAGE BODY

### What I did

**The website.** A Squarespace site built for a first-time self-publishing author, with a books page that uses parallax to give readers something closer to an experience than a catalog. Designed so Gabriel could keep adding to it as the series grows, rather than needing me every time a new book comes out.

**Brand and social.** Research into how authors in his space present themselves, then a set of social templates and assets so his brand reads the same on every platform.

**A how-to guide.** A custom guide written for him specifically, covering how to update pages, add books, and expand the site over time. This was as much of the deliverable as the design was. He came in with very little web design knowledge, and the goal was for him to leave able to run it himself.
