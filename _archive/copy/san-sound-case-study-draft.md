## PAGE BODY

### Overview

SAN Sound was a startup building SANWEAR, a line of wireless in-ear monitors. I came on as a co-founder and product designer, and over two years the role became whatever the company needed that week. Ultimately, I was the person who took an idea and made it real, buildable, and shippable.

### Context

Startups don't hand out narrow job descriptions. We had a lead designer, a collaborating artist on packaging graphics, and a small team where everyone stretched. My work sat at the seam between design and production. I took concepts from the team and turned them into files a factory could actually manufacture from, then handled whatever else needed a designer: packaging, manuals, merch, marketing emails, social, and the first versions of our web and app before we brought on specialists who took those further than I could have.

### What I owned

**Production translation.** I took headphone designs and produced the manufacturer-ready files: scaled OPGs down to sub-millimeter tolerances, Pantone specifications, material and finish callouts, dieline specs. I communicated directly with production to get them built correctly, across revision cycles and time zones.

**Packaging and manuals.** The SANWEAR-GT box, inside and out: guiding stickers, ear tip and sport hook trays, foil-stamped collab sleeves. Plus the quick start guides through multiple versions, including the regulatory pages: FCC IDs, CE compliance, battery and hearing safety, environmental disposal.

**Partner collaborations.** I authored the design proposal decks for partner SANWEAR editions, presenting multiple colorway directions per partner and carrying the chosen direction through to production files. Produced editions included ComplexCon, TV Chosun, Kindly, and Supra. I also designed concept proposals for several other partners that never went to production.

**Merch and apparel.** GT hats, t-shirts, polos, workwear, half-zips: mockups, templates, and print-ready files with labels and hangtags.

**Marketing and social.** Design and layout for the SANCTUARY newsletter in Klaviyo, including A/B variants. Social graphics and short-form video (a portion of the team's output, not all of it).

**Early web and app.** I designed the first versions of our site pages and app concepts. Specialists later took these on and improved them well past what I built.

### What I learned

How to hold a design to a spec sheet. Millimeters matter. Pantone codes matter. A logo that reads beautifully on screen can be unprintable at 7mm on a curved plastic shell, and finding out which is the job. I learned to design backwards from what a factory can physically do.

I also learned what it means to be the generalist on a small team. There's no version of this job where you only do the part you're best at. You do the part that's blocking everyone else.

### Outcome

By the time I left in 2024, SANWEAR was in the hands of a community across 37 countries. The SANWEAR-GT launched with the packaging, manuals, and production files I built, and the partner editions with ComplexCon, TV Chosun, Kindly, and Supra went to production.
