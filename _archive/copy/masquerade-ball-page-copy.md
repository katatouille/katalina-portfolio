## OPTIONAL SHORT BODY

*Only if you want more than a caption. Two paragraphs maximum on a personal project.*

Club Nothing is Nothing's nightlife grant, giving four music collectives $10,000 each to throw their biggest event yet. Applications are judged on innovative creativity, rebellious sound, and community values, and also on whether you can actually run the thing: event history, logistics, feasibility, budget.

So the proposal had to do two jobs at once. It had to feel like the party, uninhibited, celestial, romantic, somewhere between 1996 Romeo + Juliet and 2006 Marie Antoinette, while also reading as a plan someone could hand $10,000 to without worrying. Designing something that is both a mood and a budget document is the actual problem here.
